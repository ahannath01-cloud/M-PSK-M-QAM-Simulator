QPSK, 8-PSK, 16-QAM and 64-QAM with average symbol energy normalized to Es = 1, Gray and natural-binary labelings, and minimum-Euclidean-distance detection over complex AWGN. Constellation snapshots use Es/N0; BER/SER curves use Eb/N0 = Es/(N0·log2M).

Expected: with Es fixed, packing more points in the same energy shrinks the minimum distance — dmin = 2 sin(π/M) for M-PSK and √(6/(M−1)) for square M-QAM — so higher-order schemes need more Eb/N0 for the same BER but carry more bits per symbol (spectral efficiency log2M). 64-QAM clouds at 5 dB overlap completely; at 20 dB they separate. Gray labeling makes adjacent-symbol errors cost one bit, so its BER should approach SER/log2M, while binary labeling is noticeably worse for M ≥ 8.
